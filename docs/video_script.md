# Ten-minute demonstration plan

0:00-1:00 introduce the marketplace and database split. 1:00-3:00 log in as
customer, search, open a product and show the MariaDB/SQLite fields plus rating.
3:00-4:30 add to cart and complete simulated checkout. 4:30-6:00 save/delete a
review and show browsing history. 6:00-7:30 switch to seller and update stock.
7:30-9:00 switch to admin and show revenue, category sales, low-stock and views.
9:00-10:00 explain schema, transaction rollback, limitations and future
MariaDB/MongoDB deployment.
